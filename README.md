# SAFT-IMP
FIles from the manuscript " Large language models as collaborators in molecular thermodynamics: a case study of the LLM-assisted development of SAFT-VR IMP, an equation of state for polar fluids" by Werner A. Müller, Benoit Creton, Carlos Nieto-Draghi, and Erich A. Müller.
Files include:
- SAFT-VR IMP codes and patches in Julia to deploy in Clapeyron
- Transcript of the prompts and conversation with the LLM
- Validation files for the appendix
